Care for your parent, close to Baner

Many families in Baner and Balewadi moved here for work, and their parents moved with them or came later. When a parent needs more help than a busy household can give, the hardest part is often distance: a good home on the other side of the city means visiting only on weekends.

Both of our homes in this part of Pune sit on the Pashan–Sus Road, a short drive apart, so you can see both before choosing the one that suits your parent.

Areas we serve from these homes

  • BanerAnanda Nivas is in Baner itself, behind the 52 Green Woods apartments.

  • PashanBoth homes are on the Pashan–Sus Road that starts here.

Medical support

Day-to-day medical care happens inside the home. When a hospital is needed, we arrange the transfer and keep you informed.

  • Nurses on every shiftVitals, medicines, wound and continence care, day and night.
  • Regular doctor visitsPhysicians review each resident and adjust treatment.
  • Oxygen, BiPAP and suctionFor COPD and other breathing conditions, under supervision.
  • Ryle's tube and PEG feedingFor residents who cannot eat or swallow safely.
  • In-house physiotherapyFor stroke, Parkinson's, fractures and general mobility.
  • Tests without a hospital tripBlood samples and X-rays taken at the home through partner labs.
  • Pharmacy partnerMedicines delivered and managed for each resident.
  • Ambulance on callFor emergencies and hospital transfers.
