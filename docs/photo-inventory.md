# Photo inventory from the old website

No photos were copied. The posts' own images are stock pictures with unknown licences, so they're left out. The photos below look like real photos of Swarnavihar. Before any is used:

1. Confirm Swarnavihar took it and owns it.
2. For anyone recognisable (especially residents), get **written consent** to publish. Health care is a sensitive context.
3. Prefer photos of rooms, food, buildings and activities where residents can't be identified.

Then send the chosen files (or tell me which ones) and they'll replace the illustrations on the Gallery, area and facilities pages, with proper alt text. See `docs/photography-brief.md` for the photos the site still needs.
