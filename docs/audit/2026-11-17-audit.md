## What changed

**Fixes**
- Favicon, Apple touch icon and web manifest added (`src/app/icon.svg`, `apple-icon.png`, `favicon.ico`, `manifest.ts`).
- The privacy link under the form is now underlined.
- The floating WhatsApp button now sits inside a labelled landmark.
- Long words in headings wrap instead of pushing small phones sideways.
- HSTS and a strict CSP: only our own origin plus Google Analytics and Cloudflare Turnstile.

**Visual upgrade ("premium evolution", same palette and fonts)**
- **Real photography:** 24 photos from the old site, optimised with location data stripped, in `public/photos/` and listed in `src/content/photos.ts`. Every image slot now shows a real photo, and illustrations remain only where no photo exists (meals, the photo sets for individual homes).
- **Photo hero:** the home's building behind a warm dark wash, cream headline, white and outline buttons, four reassurance ticks, and the area picker raised on a white card.
- **Depth:** two warm shadow tokens. Care-service, "why families choose us" and review cards are now raised cards that lift on hover, and photos zoom slowly on hover.
- **Motion:** sections fade up as they scroll into view, and the headline numbers count up. Both are switched off for visitors who prefer reduced motion, and nothing is ever hidden without JavaScript.
- **New photo sections:** the homepage medical band has a strip of three photos (breathing support, physiotherapy, ambulance), the introduction has a photo pair, the enquiry form sits beside a photo, and the gallery has 15 real photos.
- **Inner pages:** each page hero photo has a soft shadow and a brass offset frame.

**Safeguards**
- **Consent gate:** the 14 photos with recognisable residents or staff are marked `placeholder`, so the production build stays blocked until the owner confirms written consent (or removes them).
- **No condition pairing:** service pages and health articles only ever show places (gardens, rooms), never people, so no resident is implied to have dementia, paralysis and so on.
- **Pricing-tone test:** `tests/content.test.ts` now fails if our own copy contains ₹ figures, "per month" amounts or price-tier words (premium, luxury, exclusive, high-end, five-star…).
