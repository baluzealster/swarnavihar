Lead text, 21px. For the one sentence under a heading that a worried son or daughter will actually read.

Body text is 19px on desktop and 18px on phones, at 1.6 line height and no more than 68 characters a line. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next separates easily confused letters like I, l and 1, which matters when the reader is 75 and using a tablet.

Headings: Literata 500. Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400/700. No all-caps labels, no italics for emphasis. Pinch-zoom always allowed.

How admission works

  1. Call or WhatsApp

    Tell us about your parent. We'll suggest the nearest home with space.

  2. Visit the home

    See the rooms and meet the people who would care for your parent.

  3. Care assessment

    Our doctor reviews medical records and agrees a care plan with you.

  4. Move in

    We help your parent settle in and keep you updated.
