Six homes, one way of caring

Swarnavihar runs six old age homes, five across Pune and one in Bengaluru. Each is a house in a residential neighbourhood, with its own name on the gate.

Inside, older parents get what they need as they age: help with daily living, nurses on every shift, regular doctor visits and physiotherapy, without having to live in a hospital.

Visiting other homes too?

Ask every home these questions, including us. The answers tell you more than any brochure.

  • Ask how many nurses and attendants are on duty at night, not just in the day.
  • Ask to see a written care plan, and who updates it.
  • Find out what happens in an emergency at 2 a.m., and which hospital they go to.
  • Look at a resident's room, not only the show room.
  • Ask what is included in the monthly fee and what is charged extra.
  • Stay for a meal.

Medical and nursing support, without moving to a hospital

Most of what your parent needs day to day is handled inside the home, by people who know them. When a hospital is needed, we arrange the transfer.

  • Nurses on every shift

    Vitals, medicines, wound and continence care, day and night.

  • Regular doctor visits

    Physicians review each resident and adjust treatment.

  • Oxygen, BiPAP and suction

    For COPD and other breathing conditions, under supervision.

  • Ryle's tube and PEG feeding

    For residents who cannot eat or swallow safely.

  • In-house physiotherapy

    For stroke, Parkinson's, fractures and general mobility.

  • Tests without a hospital trip

    Blood samples and X-rays taken at the home through partner labs.

  • Pharmacy partner

    Medicines delivered and managed for each resident.

  • Ambulance on call

    For emergencies and hospital transfers.

Questions families ask us

If yours isn't here, ask us on WhatsApp or call any of our homes.

How much does it cost each month?

It depends on the room and on how much nursing your parent needs. Call us with their medical details and we'll give you a written estimate.

Can we visit whenever we like?

Visiting hours to be confirmed by each home.

What happens in a medical emergency?

The nurse on duty responds first, the doctor is called, and our ambulance partner transfers your parent to the nearest hospital you have chosen.

Can my parent come for a short stay first?

Short-stay and respite policy to be confirmed.

Do you accept residents with dementia?

Yes. To be confirmed per home, along with the level of dementia care each home provides.
