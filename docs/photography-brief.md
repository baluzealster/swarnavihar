# Photography brief

Until these are shot, each slot shows an illustration from the house set (`src/components/ui/Illustration.tsx`), so the site can launch without them. Photos will still lift it further. The design depends on real photographs of real Swarnavihar homes. Stock photos of foreign care homes would undo the trust the rest of the site builds.

## Style
- Natural daylight, warm but true colour, no heavy filters.
- Real residents and staff, **with written consent** (a template release form is needed per person).
- Candid moments over posed smiles; eye-level, not looking down at residents.
- Show dignity: residents dressed, hair done, in their own clothes.
- Clean, uncluttered rooms. Remove medical waste, personal documents and visible medicine labels.
- Landscape originals of at least 3000 px on the long edge. Name the files by home and subject.
